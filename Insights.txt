INSIGHT 1 — REVENUE LEADERSHIP
• West region is the top revenue contributor at 35.5% (₹843M), followed by East
  at 30% (₹713M). Together they represent 65.5% of total revenue. North (13.1%)
  is significantly underpenetrated and represents the highest growth opportunity.

INSIGHT 2 — ELECTRONICS DOMINANCE
• Electronics is the single largest category at ₹1.46B (61% of total revenue).
  iPhone 15 Pro, MacBook Air M3, and Samsung Galaxy S24 alone drive ₹632M.
  These 3 products represent 26.6% of all company revenue from 7.6% of orders.

INSIGHT 3 — DIGITAL CHANNEL SHIFT
• Online's revenue share grew from 35.3% (Jan 2023) to 54.9% (Dec 2025) —
  a 19.6 percentage-point shift in 3 years. Online surpassed offline in revenue
  for the first time in June 2025. This structural shift demands immediate
  investment in digital infrastructure and fulfillment capability.

INSIGHT 4 — RISING CATEGORIES
• Beauty (+9.1%), Fashion (+8.8%), and Electronics (+7.8%) are the three
  fastest-growing categories by 3-year CAGR. These verticals should receive
  25-30% larger assortment expansion and priority digital advertising placement
  in the Q3 campaign calendar.

INSIGHT 5 — FALLING CATEGORIES
• Appliances (-7.7%) and Stationery (-7.4%) are in confirmed structural decline.
  Recommended action: reduce SKU count by 35%, halt new product onboarding in
  these categories, and reallocate shelf space and marketing budget to
  Beauty and Sports which show superior momentum.

INSIGHT 6 — LOYAL CUSTOMER GOLDMINE
• 6,251 customers (52.1% of 12,000-customer base) purchased more than 10 times,
  generating disproportionately high lifetime value. Top 10 customers alone
  contributed ₹15.3M in total spend. A tiered VIP loyalty programme (Bronze /
  Silver / Gold / Platinum) can extend tenure and increase purchase frequency.

INSIGHT 7 — SEASONAL REVENUE SPIKES
• October-December consistently outperforms all other quarters by 18-25%
  (Diwali, Christmas, Year-End). Pre-stocking 45 days ahead and launching
  targeted promotions in Week 1 of October can capture early-season demand
  and reduce stockout risk on high-velocity SKUs.
