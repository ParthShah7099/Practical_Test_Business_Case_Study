  EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Over the 36-month analysis period, the company executed 150,000 transactions
generating ₹2.376 Billion in gross revenue across 4 regions, 10 product
categories, and 2 sales channels. The business demonstrates strong fundamentals
with consistent monthly revenue averaging ₹66M, a growing online channel
(35% → 55% share), and a deeply loyal customer base where 52% of customers
repurchased 10+ times.

Three strategic imperatives emerge from this analysis:
  1. PROTECT AND GROW West/East markets while aggressively expanding North
  2. INVEST in Beauty, Fashion, and Sports — the growth verticals
  3. ACCELERATE digital transformation to capture the online revenue majority

  FINDING 1 — REVENUE PERFORMANCE & TRAJECTORY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Total Revenue      : ₹2,376,584,898  (~₹237.7 Crore)
Avg Monthly Revenue: ₹66,016,247
Peak Month         : Oct 2025 — ₹72.3M (+9.4% above monthly average)
Lowest Month       : Feb 2023 — ₹57.6M (post-January dip, expected pattern)
Average Order Value: ₹15,844 (grew 6.3% from ₹15,498 in Jan 2023)
Total Units Sold   : ~243,000 units

Revenue grew at an implied CAGR of approximately 3.5% annually when controlling
for seasonality — below-target for a high-growth retail environment. Management
should set a 12-15% revenue growth target for 2026 through the levers identified
in this report.

RECOMMENDATION: Implement a monthly revenue target dashboard with traffic-light
KPI indicators. Set monthly targets at ₹75M for H1 2026 and ₹85M for H2 2026.

  Region  | Revenue (₹M) | Share % | Avg Order Value | Transactions
  ---------|--------------|---------|-----------------|-------------
  West     | ₹843.2M      | 35.5%   | ₹18,750         | 44,971
  East     | ₹712.6M      | 30.0%   | ₹16,957         | 42,026

West is the premium market: highest AOV at ₹18,750 indicates affluent customers
willing to pay for premium products. East is the volume market: 42K transactions
but lower AOV. South and North are opportunity markets.

ACTION: West — defend with premium product launches + exclusive brand tie-ups.
        North — invest ₹5-8M in regional marketing over 6 months. Target 15%
        revenue lift bringing North share to 15.1% (+₹47M incremental revenue).
        South — deploy value bundles and EMI schemes to increase AOV by 12%.

  FINDING 4 — DIGITAL CHANNEL STRATEGY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Online revenue share trajectory:
  Jan 2023: 35.3%  →  Jun 2024: 48.2%  →  Jun 2025: 50.7%  →  Dec 2025: 54.9%

Online crossed offline for the first time in June 2025 — a historic inflection
point. Online revenue is growing at ~7% YoY vs offline at ~1% YoY.

PAYMENT MODE DISTRIBUTION:
  UPI          : 35%  ← dominant and growing (mobile-first India)
  Credit Card  : 28%  ← high AOV transactions, reward programme potential
  Cash         : 22%  ← declining, primarily offline
  Net Banking  : 15%  ← declining, being displaced by UPI

ACTIONS:
  • Invest ₹10-15M in e-commerce platform enhancement (speed, personalization)
  • Build UPI-first checkout experience with 1-click repeat purchase
  • Launch credit card co-branded rewards for orders >₹25,000
  • Reduce cash handling infrastructure by 20% as cash share declines
